My City...

Tehran is my hometown and the capital city of Iran, located at the foot of the Alborz Mountains. It is a city where history, culture, and daily life are closely connected. Growing up in Tehran has allowed me to experience both traditional Iranian values and a modern urban lifestyle. The city is known for its busy streets, diverse neighborhoods, and cultural spaces such as museums, parks, and universities. For me, Tehran is not just my home but a city full of good memories and love...

When I was little, I loved going to the park near my house in Tehran. I remember running around with my friends, feeling the cool breeze from the mountains, and listening to the birds singing. Sometimes my family would take me to the local bazaar, and I loved looking at all the colorful spices and sweets. Those moments made me feel happy and safe, and they are the memories I always think about when I remember my city.

Best Places to Visit in Tehran

  • Golestan Palace – a historic royal complex with beautiful gardens and architecture.
  • Milad Tower – one of the tallest towers in the world, offering a great view of the city.
  • Darband – a peaceful mountain area perfect for hiking and enjoying nature.
